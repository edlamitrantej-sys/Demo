THE MODEL BEHIND THE ROUTE

From a photo to a confident decision.

A practical plan for the full hackathon deliverable. The current page is the interactive front end; real scores appear after you train and evaluate on labeled images.

01
▧

Prepare the images

Use a small, balanced set of labeled examples. Correct orientation, convert to RGB, resize consistently, normalize, and augment training images with small rotations, crops, and brightness changes.

02
◫

Compare two models

Set a simple baseline with resized pixels and a linear classifier. Compare it with a pretrained image CNN whose final layer is fine-tuned for the four document types.

03
⌁

Measure what matters

Keep a held-out test set. Report accuracy, macro-F1, and a confusion matrix so every category’s mistakes are visible—not just the overall score.

04
↗

Route with a safety net

Calibrate the confidence threshold on validation data. Reject blurry or unsupported files, and send low-confidence or unfamiliar documents to human review.
